# Bullet combat

The player automatically fires visible homing shots at the closest threat. Damage, kills, core, and boss defeat are recorded when a shot hits, rather than when it is emitted. Player and boss stats still come from the existing battle session and stage responses.

Enemies enter from the top, right, bottom, and left and pursue the player, who starts in the center. Green security robots have 12 HP, move at 32 units per second, deal 3 damage on contact, and never fire bullets. Bosses alternate a nine-shot fan toward the player with a fan plus three faster aimed shots. Boss sprites brighten before firing. Automatic fire selects the nearest enemy by distance in both axes.

Controls: drag in any direction inside the arena or hold WASD / arrow keys. Space or the two dash buttons dash horizontally. A dash moves 100 units, grants 0.25 seconds of invulnerability (extended by the `duration` bonus), and has a 0.75-second cooldown. **Q**, **E**, or the center **궁극기** button between the dash buttons fires the ultimate (see below). The bright center on the player indicates the small collision area. Both contact and projectile hits share 0.4 seconds of protection and flash the player, preventing overlapping enemies from multiplying damage in the same frame.

BattleRun limits each simulation step to 0.1 seconds, checks swept projectile segments against the collision radius in both axes, expires offscreen/old bullets, and bounds the total bullet population at 256. Movement is limited to 360 units per second, including diagonals, within X ±160 and Y ±180. The compact combat layout keeps the arena, both dash buttons, and session controls visible together at the portrait reference size.

For local testing in Unity, open Bootstrap and select **Idle Game > Verify Bullet Patterns**. **Idle Game > Preview Bullet Battle** starts an Editor-only practice battle labeled as granting no rewards; it does not contact the server. This preview is not available in player builds. It exits to login with the practice exit button or when its duration/health runs out.

The first half contains security robots only. At 50% progress, blue rifle robots join and fire one aimed shot every 2.2 seconds. At 70%, larger white armored robots join with 60 base HP, slower movement, and 6 contact damage. At 80%, red machine-gun robots join with 36 base HP and six-shot bursts followed by a 2.6-second reload; each burst holds its initial aim. Rifle robots have 24 base HP. Newly spawned enemies gain health continuously during the second half, reaching three times base HP at the end. Existing enemies keep their spawn-time health. Boss timing remains the later of the server timing and the halfway point. The verification menu covers this progression alongside movement, contact, bullets, and rewards. Device/Android gameplay and real server completion remain separate integration checks.

## In-battle upgrade selection

When a progress report (`POST api/battles/{sessionId}/progress`) returns a non-null `pendingOffer`, or `upgradesAvailable: true` without one, `ClientApp` pauses the simulation (`battlePausedForUpgrade`: no input, no `BattleRun.Tick`) and opens a modal inside the 540 x 960 portrait safe area. If the response carries no offer, the client calls `POST api/battles/{sessionId}/upgrades/offer` with a stable `Idempotency-Key`; a 404 without a code (or `ROUTE_NOT_FOUND`) from a server lacking the upgrade endpoints leaves the battle running and skips the modal, while codes such as `SESSION_NOT_FOUND` are reported as errors. A `pendingOffer` at or below the already resolved level is ignored; a bare `upgradesAvailable` is ignored only when its `battleLevel` is older than the resolved level, so the next level-up after a pick still opens an offer. Once the battle has ended, only an unresolved `pendingOffer` opens the modal and the finish request waits for the pick. If finishing is rejected with `PENDING_UPGRADES`, the client reloads the offer and shows the modal.

The modal shows up to three cards with name, description, tier / category, and a summary of the main effects. Tapping a card sends `POST api/battles/{sessionId}/upgrades/select` with `{ selectedUpgradeCode }`. The pick keeps one `Idempotency-Key` until the request succeeds or the server rejects it with a 4xx, so a retry after a timeout resends the same pick instead of applying a second upgrade. The modal also offers a button to reload the offer.

On success `BattleRun.ApplyEffectiveStats(effectiveStats, selectedUpgrade)` applies the server's stats. Effects that change the simulation: attack power; `attackSpeed` ms to a fire interval (at least 0.05 s); `moveSpeed` as a percentage of the 360 units per second baseline; `dashCooldown` ms scaled against the 5000 ms / 0.75 s baseline (at least 0.2 s); max HP (the gained amount also heals); `hpRegenPerSec` (regeneration every simulation step); `shieldHp` (the increase is added to the current shield, and shields absorb hit damage before HP); `healOnKill` (the server maps a card's `healAmount` to `effectiveStats.healOnKill`, and `BattleRun.DefeatEnemy()` heals that amount on each kill, capped at max HP); `critRate` (percent chance per hit) and `critDamage` (extra percent on top of the 1.5x base crit multiplier), rolled from a separate seeded random so enemy spawn patterns stay unchanged; and `projectileSpeed` (a percentage added to the 620 units per second friendly shot speed, taken from the picked card's effects or from effectiveStats when the server reports a total). Session `loadoutSnapshot.bonuses.shieldHp` / `hpRegenPerSec` (module bonuses, as an object or a JSON string) apply from the first tick, and later effectiveStats values are treated as totals. Every effect field in the server's upgrade data is now simulated, including the six combat effects `ultimatePower`, `ultimateCooldown`, `splashRadius`, `duration`, `chainTargets`, and `bombRadius` described below. The HUD shows the applied upgrade count, the ultimate cooldown, and any remaining shield. Finishing, abandoning, or leaving the practice battle clears the modal, the pause, and the temporary keys.

### Ultimate and combat effects

**Ultimate.** `statSnapshot.ultimatePower` (default 30) and `ultimateCooldown` (default 30000 ms, converted to seconds with a 3 s floor) initialize `BattleRun.UltimatePower` / `UltimateCooldownDuration`; effectiveStats totals replace them after each pick. The ultimate is ready at the start of the battle. `BattleRun.TriggerUltimate()` (Q, E, or the button) returns false while on cooldown or after the battle ended; otherwise it deals `UltimatePower` damage to every living enemy (bosses included, kills and boss defeats are recorded as usual), grants 0.35 seconds of invulnerability, increments `UltimatesUsed`, and starts the cooldown. The button caption reads `궁극기 준비` or `궁극기 N.N초` and the button is disabled while cooling down, paused for an upgrade, or after the battle ended; each use shows a 180 x 180 explosion at the pilot.

**Other combat effects** (all fixed, deterministic, and never drawing from the enemy-spawn random):

- `splashRadius`: a friendly shot that hits its target deals 50% of the shot's damage to other living enemies within `splashRadius * 24` of the impact. It does not trigger further splash or chain.
- `chainTargets`: a hit also strikes up to that many other enemies within 140 units of the impact, nearest first, for 60% damage. It does not re-chain.
- `bombRadius`: a defeated enemy explodes for 80% of attack power to remaining enemies within `bombRadius * 28`. Explosion kills can chain, capped at three levels.
- `duration` (percent): scales dash invulnerability (0.25 s) and ultimate invulnerability (0.35 s), and a shot that leaves its target alive ignites it for `1.5 * (1 + duration/100)` seconds at 25% of attack power per second. Burning enemies are tinted orange.

Like `projectileSpeed`, `splashRadius`, `chainTargets`, `bombRadius`, and `duration` use effectiveStats totals when the server reports the key, and only fall back to adding the picked card's `effects` deltas for servers that omit it. The practice battle's local pool includes ultimate, splash, chain, kill-explosion, and duration cards so each effect can be tried in **Preview Bullet Battle**.

The Editor practice battle (**Idle Game > Preview Bullet Battle**) grants 15 core per kill and offers a local three-card choice each time core energy reaches 50, 100, 150, and so on, so the ultimate, splash, chain, kill-explosion, and duration cards are all offered by the fifth level (250 core) within the 60-second run. It builds the effective stats locally and never contacts the server.

The hangar shows the current combat stats from `GET api/mecha/stats` when the server provides them, and falls back silently otherwise. Research labeled live is limited to the codes the server applies (`prod_eff_1`, `prod_eff_2`, `prod_passive`, `prod_cap`, `util_claim_bonus`, `util_idle_reward`, `econ_upgrade_discount_1`, `econ_upgrade_discount_2`, `combat_atk_1`, `combat_atk_2`, `combat_hp_1`, `combat_hp_2`, `combat_speed`, `combat_ult`, `econ_scrap`, `econ_battle_reward`).
